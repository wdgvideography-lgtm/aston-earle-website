- Freight forwarding and haulage company
- UK & European logistics brokerage
- Colors: Deep maroon/burgundy, gold/amber, warm cream
- Values: Integrity, Relationships, Accountability, Results
- Tagline: "LOGISTICS. DONE PROPERLY."
- Premium, trustworthy, established feel

## Idea 1: Industrial Heritage Luxury

**Design Movement**: Neo-Industrial meets British Heritage — think Rolls-Royce brochure meets industrial warehouse aesthetics.

**Core Principles**:
1. Contrast between raw industrial textures and refined typography
2. Warm metallics (gold, brass, copper) against deep dark surfaces
3. Deliberate asymmetry that suggests confidence and movement
4. Generous negative space that communicates premium positioning

**Color Philosophy**: Deep maroon (#3D0C11) as the anchor of authority and tradition. Burnished gold (#C9A84C) for accents that suggest value and reliability. Warm ivory (#F5F0E8) for breathing room. Charcoal (#2A2A2A) for grounding text. The palette evokes trust, heritage, and premium service.

**Layout Paradigm**: Split-panel compositions where imagery bleeds into content areas. Diagonal section dividers suggesting forward motion. Content blocks that overlap slightly, creating depth. Full-width hero with offset text positioning.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Gold-foil style accent lines and dividers that reference the brand's wing motif
2. Subtle parallax on background textures (concrete, brushed metal)
3. Large oversized section numbers in a display font as wayfinding

**Interaction Philosophy**: Smooth, weighted transitions that feel like heavy machinery in motion — deliberate, powerful, unstoppable. Hover states that reveal additional depth (shadows deepening, elements lifting).

**Animation**: Sections fade and slide in from the direction of travel (left to right). Gold accent lines draw themselves on scroll. Numbers count up when statistics enter viewport. Contact form fields have a satisfying weight when focused.

**Typography System**: Display: "Playfair Display" (serif, bold) for headlines — evokes heritage and authority. Body: "DM Sans" for body text — clean, modern, highly readable. Accent: Uppercase tracking for labels and categories.

## Idea 2: Kinetic Cartography

**Design Movement**: Data-visualization aesthetics meets logistics mapping — inspired by air traffic control interfaces and premium shipping dashboards.

**Core Principles**:
1. Route-line motifs that connect sections like waypoints on a map
2. Dark mode interface with luminous accent points
3. Information hierarchy through scale and luminosity
4. Grid-based precision suggesting operational excellence

**Color Philosophy**: Near-black navy (#0D1117) as the canvas — like a night-time logistics dashboard. Amber/gold (#D4A853) as route markers and active states. Cool slate (#94A3B8) for secondary information. White (#FFFFFF) for primary text that glows against the dark. The palette suggests 24/7 operations, precision, and technological sophistication.

**Layout Paradigm**: Modular card grid with connecting lines between sections. Fixed sidebar navigation with progress indicators. Content sections as "stations" along a visual route. Map-like overview with zoomable detail areas.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Animated dotted lines connecting sections (like freight routes)
2. Glowing node points at section intersections
3. Coordinate-style labels (subtle lat/long aesthetic for section markers)

**Interaction Philosophy**: Precise, snappy interactions like clicking waypoints on a map. Elements illuminate on hover like dashboard indicators activating. Scrolling reveals the "route" progressively.

**Animation**: Route lines draw progressively as user scrolls. Cards slide in from their geographic direction. Pulsing glow effects on CTAs. Smooth counter animations for statistics.

**Typography System**: Display: "Space Grotesk" — geometric, technical, modern. Body: "Inter" at 400/500 weights for dashboard clarity. Monospace: "JetBrains Mono" for numbers and data points.

## Idea 3: Warm Authority

**Design Movement**: Contemporary British Corporate — think premium law firm website meets modern logistics. Inspired by Savile Row tailoring applied to digital: structured, bespoke, warm.

**Core Principles**:
1. Warmth through materials — leather textures, warm paper tones, brass accents
2. Strong vertical rhythm and generous line heights for readability
3. Restrained elegance — every element earns its place
4. Trust through consistency and predictability of layout

**Color Philosophy**: Rich burgundy (#5C1A1B) as the brand pillar — authority without aggression. Antique gold (#B8963E) for calls-to-action and highlights — valuable, earned. Warm stone (#F7F3ED) as the primary background — like quality stationery. Deep espresso (#1C1410) for text — warmer than pure black, more sophisticated. The palette communicates established expertise and personal service.

**Layout Paradigm**: Classical proportions with a modern twist — wide content areas with generous margins. Alternating full-width and contained sections. Left-aligned content with right-floating imagery. Sticky navigation that transforms on scroll from transparent to solid.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Subtle paper/linen texture on backgrounds
2. Thin gold horizontal rules as section dividers (referencing the brand's wing lines)
3. Oversized pull quotes in serif italic for testimonials and brand statements

**Interaction Philosophy**: Refined and measured — like a well-tailored suit. Nothing flashy, everything intentional. Buttons have subtle depth on hover. Forms feel like filling in premium paperwork.

**Animation**: Gentle fade-ups on scroll (200ms, ease-out). Navigation transitions smoothly between states. Form validation appears with a soft slide. Page transitions use a brief warm-toned overlay.

**Typography System**: Display: "Cormorant Garamond" (serif) — elegant, authoritative, warm. Body: "Source Sans 3" — professional, highly legible, friendly. Accent: Uppercase "Source Sans 3" with wide tracking for labels.
